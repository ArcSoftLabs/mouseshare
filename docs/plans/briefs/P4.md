## Requirements
1. **First outbound frame is encoded at `MIN_VERSION` (2) and carries `max_v: VERSION`.** Only the connector's first frame (`pair_request`) does this; a v2 peer ignores the unknown `max_v` field (verify against `git show v0.2.0:mouseshare/protocol.py` that unknown keys are tolerated — they are, decode only checks `v` and `t`).
2. **Responder pins `min(VERSION, max_v or v)`** on that first inbound frame and replies at the pinned version (so new↔new negotiates to 3, new→old stays 2, old→new: the old connector sends `v:2` without `max_v` → pinned 2).
3. **Connector pins on its first inbound frame's `v`** (the responder's reply); its later frames use that version. Fix `app.py:443` so a new↔new session is not pinned to 2 by the v2-encoded first frame.
4. **Pin rule exception:** the M-A rule "a later frame whose `v` differs from the pinned version is a ProtocolError" keeps applying to *inbound* frames after the pin; the connector's own v2 first frame is outbound and exempt by construction. Confirm the downgrade test in `tests/test_security.py` still fails if the app-level pin is removed, and add: a responder that receives `pair_request v:2 max_v:3` then a v2-encoded `auth` (an attacker trying to stay at 2 after negotiating 3) is torn down.
5. No change to `pair_ok` authentication semantics: v2-negotiated sessions still get the warning path; v3 requires `hmac`.

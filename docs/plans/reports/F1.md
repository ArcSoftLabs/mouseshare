Receiver sequence:

1. Validate all metadata and available space.
2. Choose collision-free destination names.
3. Open `.part` exclusively and stream chunks into it.
4. Flush/fsync and verify byte count plus SHA-256.
5. Atomically rename only if the final destination remains unused.

Flow control: the sender queues at most eight chunks before waiting for `xfer_ack`; the receiver acknowledges every eight chunks and the final chunk. This lets queued position traffic run between bursts.
